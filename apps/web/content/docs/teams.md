---
title: Teams
description: Shared workspaces, roles, invites, ownership transfer, and team deletion.
---

Teams let multiple people share projects in one workspace. Use them when diagrams are owned by a group rather than a single person.

## Create a team

1. Open the workspace switcher / **Teams** controls.
2. Create a team with a clear name.
3. You become the initial **owner**.

## Roles

| Role | Typical powers |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control, including membership, ownership transfer, and team deletion |
| **Admin** | Manage members and team projects |
| **Member** | Access and edit team projects according to product rules |

Exact UI labels may vary slightly; owners always retain the strongest control.

## Invite members

1. Open the team manage dialog.
2. Invite by email / account identifier.
3. Assign a role.
4. The invitee appears in the member list once accepted (or immediately, depending on invite flow).

Remove members the same way when someone leaves the company or project.

## Team projects

Projects associated with a team are visible to members. Personal projects stay private unless shared.

When collaborating:

- Prefer team ownership for long-lived architecture docs.
- Use [Sharing](/docs/sharing) for external read-only viewers.

## Transfer ownership

Owners can transfer team (or project) ownership to another member when stepping away or handing off a project.

1. Open manage / ownership controls.
2. Choose the new owner.
3. Confirm.

After transfer, the previous owner may remain as admin/member or leave, depending on what you select.

## Delete a team

Owners can permanently delete a team from the manage dialog (confirmation required). **Team projects move to Trash** — they are not wiped instantly. Restore them from [Trash](/docs/trash) if the delete was a mistake, or keep working on them as personal/trash items according to the product flow.

Prefer transferring ownership and adjusting membership when the team should survive under new leadership.

## Tips

- Export critical projects before major membership or deletion changes if you need an offline copy.
- Public share links are independent of team membership; revoke them separately under [Sharing](/docs/sharing).
