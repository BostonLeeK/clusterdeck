ClusterDeck uses account-based access. Diagrams, teams, and shares are tied to your user.

## Sign up / sign in

- **Sign up** creates a new account with email and password.
- **Sign in** restores an existing session.
- Google and GitHub sign-in are supported when configured — the same email links to one ClusterDeck account across methods.
- After authentication you are redirected to **Projects**.

Keep your password private. Prefer a unique password for this product.

## Session

While signed in you can:

- Open and edit diagrams you own or can access
- Manage teams where you are a member
- Use export, history, templates, and AI features according to your permissions

Signing out ends the browser session. Shared **public** links remain available without login.

## Profile

Your account identity appears in team membership and share invitations. Ownership of diagrams and teams can be transferred (see [Teams](/docs/teams) and [Projects](/docs/projects)).

## Permissions at a glance

| Context | Who can change it |
| --- | --- |
| Personal diagram | Owner (and invited collaborators with edit access) |
| Team diagram | Team members according to role |
| Public share | Anyone with the link (read-only) |
| Templates | Visible according to product rules; saving as template needs access to the source diagram |
